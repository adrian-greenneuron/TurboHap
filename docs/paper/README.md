# TurboHap manuscript and supplementary material

**Adrian R. Carr (2026). _TurboHap: accelerating FindHap haplotype phasing and
genotype imputation while preserving output compatibility._**

## v1.6.0 manuscript · 20 November 2026

The manuscript and supplementary methods use **TurboHap v1.6.0** for their code, figures, tables and benchmark results. Both PDFs are reproduced here as issued on 20 November 2026. The author manuscript has not been peer reviewed. [Benchmarks for the v1.7.0 software](../turbohap_benchmarks_221126.md)

| Download | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Manuscript (PDF)](v1.6.0/turbohap_manuscript_201126.pdf) | Phasing and imputation methods, six figures and two benchmark tables |
| [Supplementary methods (PDF)](v1.6.0/turbohap_supplement_201126.pdf) | Implementation details, verification methods and benchmark measurements |
| [SHA-256 checksums](v1.6.0/SHA256SUMS) | Checksums for both PDFs |

Both PDFs include an offline reader containing the cited C++ code, benchmark measurements and verification evidence. To read it offline, save `source-evidence.html` from the attachments panel in either PDF and open it in a browser. The individual measurements underlying the paper are also available in the archived release. [v1.6.0 benchmark results](https://github.com/adrian-greenneuron/TurboHap/blob/v1.6.0/docs/turbohap_benchmarks_201126.md)

## Original v1.0.0 manuscript · 22 October 2026

The October paper describes TurboHap v1.0.0 and its seven-dataset benchmark. The original manuscript, both supplements and their checksums are available in the [v1.5.0 manuscript archive](https://github.com/adrian-greenneuron/TurboHap/tree/v1.5.0/docs/paper).
